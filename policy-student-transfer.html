Student Transfer Policy

1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish clear and consistent principles for the transfer of students into or out of Baimuru Primary School.

The policy is intended to ensure that student transfers are properly authorised, documented and managed while protecting the student's educational continuity and records.

2. Policy Statement

Baimuru Primary School will manage student transfers fairly, efficiently and in accordance with applicable Papua New Guinea education legislation, National Department of Education requirements and Provincial Education requirements.

The School will seek to ensure that:

  • transfers are properly documented;
  • student records are accurately maintained;
  • students experience as little disruption to their education as reasonably possible;
  • parents and guardians are appropriately informed;
  • information is shared only with authorised persons or institutions; and
  • transfer decisions are handled consistently and transparently.

The School will not impose unauthorised transfer fees or use non-payment of fees as a reason to deny a student's right to education where prohibited by applicable Government policy. The current Government Free Education Policy expressly states that schools should not charge registration, admission or transfer fees and that students should not be denied enrolment or attendance because of non-payment of fees.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • students transferring into Baimuru Primary School;
  • students transferring from Baimuru Primary School to another school;
  • parents and guardians;
  • the Head Teacher;
  • teachers;
  • administrative staff; and
  • other persons authorised to participate in the transfer process.

This policy establishes the principles governing student transfers.

The detailed transfer process, forms and record-management requirements are contained in the relevant procedures and forms.

4. Transfer Into Baimuru Primary School

A student transferring from another school may be considered for admission subject to:

  • availability of an appropriate place;
  • verification of the student's identity and educational information;
  • appropriate transfer documentation;
  • applicable education-authority requirements; and
  • the School's enrolment and admission requirements.

The School may communicate with the student's previous school to verify relevant educational and administrative information.

Where a transfer matter requires consideration by an education authority, the School will follow the applicable authority's requirements.

5. Transfer From Baimuru Primary School

A parent or guardian seeking to transfer a student from Baimuru Primary School should notify the School and provide the information reasonably required to process the transfer.

The School will:

  • verify the student's School record;
  • complete or provide the required transfer documentation;
  • update the student's enrolment status;
  • provide authorised educational records where required; and
  • retain appropriate records of the transfer.

The School should not unnecessarily delay the legitimate transfer of a student.

6. Student Records

Student records are an important part of the transfer process.

The School will maintain accurate records of:

  • the student's enrolment;
  • grade/class placement;
  • attendance;
  • relevant academic information;
  • transfer details; and
  • other information required by the education system.

Records transferred to another school will be provided only through appropriate and authorised channels.

The management and confidentiality of student information are governed by the Student Records and Information Policy.

This policy therefore does not repeat the detailed requirements for maintaining student records.

7. Continuity of Education

Baimuru Primary School recognises that a transfer should, as far as reasonably possible, allow the student to continue learning without unnecessary interruption.

Where appropriate, the School will provide relevant information to support:

  • appropriate grade placement;
  • continuity of learning;
  • understanding of the student's educational progress; and
  • appropriate support for the student's transition.

Where a student transfers into Baimuru Primary School, the School may consider available information about the student's previous education when determining appropriate class placement.

8. Parent and Guardian Responsibilities

Parents and guardians are expected to:

  • provide accurate information;
  • notify the School of an intended transfer;
  • provide required transfer information;
  • cooperate with the completion of transfer documentation; and
  • ensure that relevant contact information is current.

Parents and guardians should communicate transfer arrangements with the School as early as reasonably possible.

9. School Responsibilities

The School will:

  • administer transfers fairly and consistently;
  • maintain accurate student records;
  • complete required documentation;
  • protect confidential information;
  • communicate appropriately with parents and guardians;
  • cooperate with other schools and authorised education authorities; and
  • maintain a record of completed transfers.

The Head Teacher is responsible for ensuring that the School's transfer system operates in accordance with this policy and applicable education requirements.

10. Transfer Documentation

The School will use the relevant National Department of Education, Provincial Education authority or School-approved transfer documentation as applicable.

The Department of Education currently provides an official Student Transfer Form for student transfers between provincial schools.

The School may maintain supporting documents such as:

  • transfer request;
  • student transfer form;
  • student record summary;
  • receiving-school confirmation;
  • transfer register; and
  • other documents required by the education authorities.

These documents form part of the School's administrative records.

11. Confidentiality

Information relating to a student's transfer must be handled confidentially.

Student information should only be disclosed to:

  • the parent or authorised guardian;
  • authorised School personnel;
  • the receiving or previous school where appropriate;
  • authorised education authorities; or
  • another person or organisation where disclosure is lawfully required or authorised.

12. Disputed or Exceptional Transfers

Where a transfer involves a dispute, uncertainty regarding a student's status, incomplete records or another exceptional circumstance, the Head Teacher may seek guidance from the appropriate education authority.

The School will seek to resolve transfer matters fairly while maintaining the student's educational interests and complying with applicable requirements.

Where a person believes that a transfer-related decision has been handled unfairly, the matter may be raised through the School's Complaints, Grievances and Appeals Policy.

13. Related Documents

This policy is supported by:

  • Student Transfer Procedure;
  • Student Transfer Form;
  • Student Transfer Register;
  • Student Enrolment and Admission Policy;
  • Student Records and Information Policy;
  • Student Attendance Policy; and
  • Complaints, Grievances and Appeals Policy.

14. Policy Authority

This policy operates within the authority of:

  • the Constitution of Papua New Guinea;
  • applicable education legislation;
  • National Department of Education policies, standards and directives;
  • relevant Provincial Education requirements;
  • the Baimuru Primary School Constitution; and
  • other applicable Government requirements.

Where this policy conflicts with a higher-level legal or education requirement, the higher-level requirement takes precedence.

15. Review

This policy will be reviewed periodically and may be reviewed earlier when there are changes to:

  • education legislation;
  • national education policy;
  • provincial education requirements;
  • School enrolment arrangements; or
  • student transfer requirements.
