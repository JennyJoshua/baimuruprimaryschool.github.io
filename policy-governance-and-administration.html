Introduction

Baimuru Primary School recognises that effective governance and administration are essential to providing quality education, maintaining a safe and supportive learning environment, and ensuring that School resources are used responsibly.

The School's governance and administrative framework establishes the structures, responsibilities, policies and procedures through which the School is governed and managed.

Baimuru Primary School operates within the broader education system of Papua New Guinea and is guided by the Constitution of Papua New Guinea, applicable education legislation, national education policies and directives, provincial education requirements, and the School's own Constitution, policies, rules and procedures.

The School's governance framework is therefore built on the principle that School-level governance must operate within the authority of the national and provincial education system.

1. Governance Hierarchy

The governance framework of Baimuru Primary School follows a clear hierarchy of authority:

  1. Constitution of Papua New Guinea
  2. Education Acts and Other Relevant Legislation
  3. National Education Policies, Standards and Directives
  4. Provincial Education Board / Provincial Education Authority
  5. Baimuru Primary School Governance Structure
  6. Baimuru Primary School Constitution
  7. School Policies
  8. School Rules
  9. Operating Procedures
  10. Forms and Records

This hierarchy provides the foundation for the development, implementation and review of the School's policies and administrative practices.

The School's policies and procedures must remain consistent with applicable laws, national education requirements and provincial directives.

2. Baimuru Primary School Governance Structure

The governance of Baimuru Primary School is carried out through the School's established governing and administrative structures.

The principal School governance and leadership bodies include:

  • the School's governing body or Board of Management;
  • the Head Teacher;
  • teaching staff;
  • administrative and support staff;
  • parents and guardians;
  • School committees and authorised representatives; and
  • the wider School community.

The exact composition and responsibilities of these bodies are determined by the applicable education legislation, provincial requirements and the School's Constitution.

Under the Education Act, a Board of Management is established for primary schools within the National Education System, with responsibilities relating to areas such as School facilities, enrolment, School aims and goals, student discipline and accountability for funds made available to the Board.

3. The School Constitution

The Baimuru Primary School Constitution provides the School's internal foundation for governance.

It establishes the basic principles under which the School's governing structures operate and provides a framework for:

  • School governance;
  • leadership;
  • responsibilities and authority;
  • meetings and decision-making;
  • accountability;
  • stakeholder participation;
  • School development; and
  • other matters relating to the governance of the School.

The School Constitution must operate consistently with applicable national and provincial education requirements.

It should therefore be regarded as the School's internal governing instrument rather than as a document that replaces national legislation or education-authority requirements.

4. Board of Management / Governing Body

The Board of Management provides an important governance link between the School, its community and the wider education system.

The Board works with the Head Teacher and School administration to support the effective operation and development of Baimuru Primary School.

Depending on the responsibilities assigned under applicable legislation and education requirements, the Board's functions may include:

  • participating in School planning and development;
  • supporting the provision and maintenance of School facilities;
  • supporting appropriate enrolment arrangements;
  • establishing or supporting School aims and goals;
  • overseeing the achievement of School objectives;
  • supporting appropriate student discipline arrangements;
  • supporting responsible financial management;
  • promoting community participation;
  • considering School development priorities;
  • supporting the implementation of approved School policies; and
  • performing other functions assigned under applicable education requirements.

The Education Act also provides that Boards of Management are accountable for funds made available to them by the State, education authorities or the public.

5. Role of the Head Teacher

The Head Teacher is responsible for the professional and day-to-day leadership and administration of Baimuru Primary School within the authority provided by the education system and the School's governance arrangements.

The Head Teacher provides leadership in areas including:

  • teaching and learning;
  • staff management;
  • student welfare and discipline;
  • implementation of School policies;
  • School planning;
  • administration;
  • financial management within delegated authority;
  • property and resource management;
  • School records;
  • communication with parents and the community;
  • implementation of governing-body decisions;
  • liaison with education authorities; and
  • monitoring the overall operation of the School.

The Head Teacher works closely with the Board of Management, teachers, staff, parents and other stakeholders to achieve the School's objectives.

These policies provide direction on matters such as:

Governance

  • decision-making;
  • delegation of authority;
  • meetings;
  • resolutions;
  • accountability; and
  • policy development and review.

School Administration

  • student administration;
  • staff administration;
  • School records;
  • correspondence;
  • reporting;
  • document management; and

Financial Administration

  • financial accountability;
  • budgeting;
  • expenditure;
  • receipting and banking;
  • fundraising;
  • donations;
  • financial reporting; and
  • acquittals.

Property and Resources

  • School property;

  • equipment;
  • inventory;

7. Relationship Between Policies, Rules and Procedures

Baimuru Primary School maintains a clear distinction between policies, rules, procedures and records.

Policies — Policies establish the School's principles, requirements and responsibilities.

School Rules — Rules establish practical expectations for students, staff and other members of the School community.

Operating Procedures — Procedures explain how particular activities are to be carried out.

Forms and Records — Forms and records provide the documentation and evidence required to support School operations and accountability.

The relationship can therefore be represented as:

  1. PolicyWhat the School requires
  2. RuleWhat members of the School community are expected to follow
  3. ProcedureHow the requirement is implemented
  4. Form / Register / RecordEvidence that the activity or decision occurred

This structure helps the School avoid unnecessary duplication and ensures that every administrative document has a clear purpose.

8. Administrative Accountability

Baimuru Primary School is committed to maintaining high standards of accountability in all areas of administration.

Persons entrusted with School responsibilities are expected to:

  • act honestly and professionally;
  • exercise authority appropriately;
  • follow approved policies and procedures;
  • maintain accurate records;
  • protect School resources;
  • maintain confidentiality;
  • declare relevant conflicts of interest;
  • properly document important decisions;
  • use School resources only for authorised purposes; and
  • accept responsibility for actions taken within their authority.

The School seeks to maintain an administrative culture based on integrity, transparency, responsibility and service to students.

9. Financial Governance and Accountability

The School recognises that financial resources must be managed carefully and transparently.

The School's financial administration includes appropriate systems for:

  • budgeting;
  • financial planning;
  • authorised expenditure;
  • purchasing;
  • receipting;
  • banking;
  • fundraising;
  • donations;
  • financial reporting;
  • acquittals; and
  • financial record keeping.

Financial responsibilities are exercised according to the authority provided by applicable education and financial requirements and the School's approved policies and procedures.

The School is committed to ensuring that financial resources are used for legitimate School purposes and in ways that support student learning and School development.

10. Property, Assets and School Resources

School property and resources are valuable assets belonging to or entrusted to the School for educational purposes.

The School maintains appropriate systems for:

  • recording assets;
  • managing equipment;
  • maintaining inventories;
  • monitoring School property;
  • issuing and returning School resources;
  • reporting loss or damage;
  • maintaining facilities;
  • conducting stocktakes;
  • undertaking repairs and maintenance; and
  • managing authorised disposal of assets.

All members of the School community share a responsibility to care for School property.

11. Records and Information Management

Good administration depends on accurate, reliable and secure records.

Baimuru Primary School maintains appropriate records relating to:

  • students;
  • staff;
  • attendance;
  • academic matters;
  • finance;
  • assets;
  • procurement;
  • meetings;
  • correspondence;
  • School projects;
  • incidents;
  • policies;
  • procedures; and
  • other official School activities.

Records may be maintained in physical and electronic formats.

The School is committed to protecting confidential information and ensuring that access to sensitive information is limited to authorised persons.

The School's developing digital information systems and database facilities will support more efficient management of School records while remaining subject to the School's information-management and confidentiality requirements.

12. Meetings and Decision-Making

Meetings are an important part of School governance and administration.

Depending on the purpose and authority involved, meetings may include:

  • Board of Management meetings;
  • staff meetings;
  • parent meetings;
  • community meetings;
  • School development meetings;
  • committee meetings; and
  • other authorised meetings.

Important decisions should be properly documented through appropriate records such as:

  • agendas;
  • attendance records;
  • minutes;
  • resolutions;
  • action items; and
  • follow-up reports.

Proper documentation supports accountability, continuity and transparency.

13. School Development and Community Participation

Baimuru Primary School recognises that School development is strengthened through cooperation between the School and its community.

The School may work with parents, guardians, community leaders, organisations, government agencies and development partners in areas such as:

  • infrastructure;
  • School facilities;
  • learning resources;
  • water and sanitation;
  • School grounds;
  • fencing and security;
  • ICT;
  • sporting facilities;
  • environmental projects; and
  • other School development priorities.

All development projects and contributions should be properly considered, authorised, recorded and managed.

The School welcomes appropriate community support while ensuring that School governance and accountability requirements are maintained.

14. Communication and Stakeholder Relations

Effective communication is an important part of good governance.

Baimuru Primary School maintains appropriate communication with:

  • students;
  • parents and guardians;
  • teachers and staff;
  • the Board of Management;
  • community leaders;
  • Provincial Education authorities;
  • National Department of Education representatives;
  • government agencies; and
  • other relevant stakeholders.

Official correspondence should be appropriately authorised and recorded.

The School also recognises the importance of communicating information to parents and the community in a clear, respectful and timely manner.

Confidential information must only be disclosed through authorised channels.

15. Policy Development, Review and Document Control

The School's governance and administrative documents are part of a controlled document system.

Policies and other controlled documents may be developed or reviewed when:

  • legislation changes;
  • national education policies change;
  • provincial requirements change;
  • School circumstances change;
  • an operational need is identified;
  • a policy reaches its review date; or
  • the School identifies an opportunity for improvement.

The School seeks to distinguish clearly between:

  1. Draft
  2. Under Review
  3. Approved
  4. Effective
  5. Superseded / Retired

This ensures that staff and stakeholders can identify the current approved version of a School document.

16. Continuous Improvement

Baimuru Primary School recognises that effective governance is an ongoing process.

The School will continue to review and improve its governance and administrative systems in order to:

  • strengthen leadership;
  • improve accountability;
  • improve financial management;
  • improve record keeping;
  • strengthen student support;
  • improve teaching and learning;
  • maintain School facilities;
  • strengthen community participation;
  • make appropriate use of technology; and
  • respond to changing educational and community needs.

The School's governance framework will therefore continue to develop in response to legislation, national and provincial education requirements, School needs and community priorities.

Our Commitment

Baimuru Primary School is committed to a governance and administration system that is:

Responsible. Transparent. Accountable. Professional. Inclusive. Community-focused. Student-centred.

Our governance and administration ultimately exist to support the core purpose of Baimuru Primary School:

To provide every child with a safe, supportive and effective learning environment in which they can learn, grow and reach their full potential.
