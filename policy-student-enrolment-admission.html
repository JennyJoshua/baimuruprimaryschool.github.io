Student Enrolment and Admission Policy

1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish clear, fair and consistent principles for the enrolment and admission of students to Baimuru Primary School.

The policy ensures that student admission is conducted in accordance with applicable Papua New Guinea education legislation, national education policies and directives, provincial requirements and the School's governance framework.

2. Policy Statement

Baimuru Primary School is committed to providing fair and equitable access to education and to maintaining an orderly and accurate enrolment system.

The School will:

  • provide admission and enrolment processes that are fair, transparent and consistent;
  • comply with national and provincial education requirements;
  • maintain accurate and up-to-date enrolment records;
  • give appropriate consideration to the age and educational needs of students;
  • support the enrolment and participation of children without unlawful discrimination;
  • protect the confidentiality of student information; and
  • work with parents, guardians and relevant education authorities to resolve enrolment matters.

The School will not refuse, restrict or remove a student from School because of non-payment of tuition or project fees where such action is prohibited by the applicable Government Free Education Policy.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • new students seeking admission;
  • students returning to the School;
  • students transferring from another School;
  • parents and guardians;
  • the Head Teacher;
  • teachers;
  • administrative staff; and
  • other persons involved in the enrolment process.

This policy establishes the principles and requirements for enrolment.

Detailed enrolment instructions, forms, checklists and record-entry procedures are maintained separately.

4. Admission Principles

Baimuru Primary School will administer admission in accordance with the following principles:

4.1 Fairness and Equality

Students will be considered for admission fairly and without unlawful discrimination.

The School will respect the rights and dignity of every child regardless of gender, disability, family circumstances, cultural background, religion, economic circumstances or other personal characteristics.

4.2 Age-Appropriate Enrolment

The School will follow applicable national requirements concerning the appropriate age and grade placement of students.

Where an enrolment matter involves an over-age, under-age or otherwise exceptional student, the School will deal with the matter in accordance with applicable education-authority requirements rather than creating its own rules.

The Department of Education has specifically directed schools to address correct-age enrolment and the appropriate handling of over-age students.

4.3 Best Interests of the Student

Admission and grade placement should take reasonable account of the student's educational needs, previous schooling, age and available information.

Where necessary, the School may seek advice from parents, guardians, teachers, inspectors or relevant education authorities.

4.4 No Unauthorised Admission Barriers

The School will not introduce admission requirements that conflict with national education legislation, policy or directives.

In particular, School fees or project fees will not be used as an unlawful condition for a child's enrolment or continued attendance under the Government Free Education Policy.

5. Enrolment Information

The School will collect the information reasonably required to establish and maintain an accurate student record.

This may include:

  • student's full name;
  • date and place of birth;
  • sex;
  • parent or guardian information;
  • contact details;
  • previous School information;
  • relevant educational information;
  • emergency contact information; and
  • other information required by the School or education authorities.

The School may request supporting documentation where required for verification or official education records.

The specific documents required will be identified in the Student Enrolment Procedure and Enrolment Form, rather than in this policy.

6. Parent and Guardian Responsibilities

Parents and guardians are expected to:

  • provide accurate information during enrolment;
  • provide required supporting information or documentation;
  • notify the School when relevant information changes;
  • provide appropriate emergency contact information;
  • support regular School attendance; and
  • communicate with the School regarding matters affecting the student's education.

Providing false or deliberately misleading information may result in the School referring the matter to the appropriate authority.

7. School Responsibilities

The School will:

  • process enrolment applications fairly and consistently;
  • maintain accurate enrolment records;
  • protect student information;
  • communicate relevant enrolment decisions appropriately;
  • provide parents or guardians with necessary information;
  • comply with applicable education requirements; and
  • ensure that enrolment information is properly entered into the School's administrative records and required education systems.

The Head Teacher is responsible for ensuring that the School's enrolment system operates effectively.

8. Admission and Grade Placement

Admission and grade placement will be determined in accordance with applicable education requirements and the information available about the student's age, previous education and educational needs.

Where a student's circumstances require special consideration, the School may consult the relevant education authority.

The School will avoid arbitrary grade placement and will maintain appropriate records supporting significant enrolment decisions.

9. Transfer Students

Students transferring from another School will be admitted and recorded in accordance with applicable transfer requirements.

The School may request relevant information from the previous School to ensure continuity of the student's educational record.

Transfer matters will be managed in accordance with the separate Student Transfer Policy and Procedure.

This prevents transfer requirements from being unnecessarily repeated in this policy.

10. Enrolment Records

The School will maintain an official enrolment record for each student.

Enrolment information must be:

  • accurate;
  • current;
  • securely maintained;
  • accessible only to authorised persons; and
  • updated when significant information changes.

The management, confidentiality and protection of student records are also governed by the School's Student Records and Information Policy.

11. Enrolment Decisions and Appeals

Where an enrolment application cannot be accepted, the School should provide the parent or guardian with an appropriate explanation where practicable.

Where a parent or guardian believes that an enrolment decision has been made unfairly or contrary to applicable requirements, the matter may be raised through the School's Complaints, Grievances and Appeals Policy and, where appropriate, referred to the relevant education authority.

12. Confidentiality

Information obtained during the enrolment process must be treated as confidential.

Student information will only be collected, used, stored or disclosed for legitimate educational, administrative, legal or safeguarding purposes and in accordance with applicable requirements.

13. Related Procedures and Documents

This policy is supported by:

  • Student Enrolment Procedure;
  • Student Enrolment Form;
  • Enrolment Checklist;
  • Student Admission/Placement Record;
  • Student Transfer Policy and Procedure;
  • Student Records and Information Policy;
  • Student Attendance Policy; and
  • Complaints, Grievances and Appeals Policy.

These documents provide the operational details necessary to implement this policy.

14. Policy Authority

This policy operates within the authority of:

  • the Constitution of Papua New Guinea;
  • applicable education legislation;
  • National Department of Education policies, standards and directives;
  • relevant Provincial Education requirements;
  • the Baimuru Primary School Constitution; and
  • other applicable Government requirements.

Where a provision of this School policy conflicts with a higher-level legal or education-authority requirement, the higher-level requirement takes precedence.

15. Review

This policy will be reviewed periodically and may be reviewed earlier where there are changes to:

  • education legislation;
  • national education policies;
  • Government Free Education requirements;
  • provincial education requirements;
  • School circumstances; or
  • enrolment and admission practices.
